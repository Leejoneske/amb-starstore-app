Earnings

What you earn, and when it reaches you.

Every ambassador earns the same 30% of the margin StarStore makes on a referred trade. There is no tier multiplier on money, and no rate to negotiate.

The rate

30%

Of StarStore's margin

On every referred order.

$0.84

Per 1,000 Stars

Bought or sold.

$0.30

Per Premium subscription

Any length.

The two figures on the right are the 30% share worked out against what StarStore actually makes on those orders. They are what the approval email quotes, and they are what the platform pays.

How a referral starts earning

200 Stars, or one Premium subscription

A referred person has to trade 200 Stars in total, buying or selling, or buy Telegram Premium. Until then the referral is pending and pays nothing. After that, every order they place earns you commission.

Attribution is permanent

Your link is a Telegram deep link. Anyone who joins StarStore through it is attributed to you for good, not for a window of days. There is no cookie to expire and nothing for the reader to keep.

Up to $50 from any one person

Commission accrues for the lifetime of a referral until it reaches $50, at which point that referral is complete and stops accruing. Your other referrals are unaffected.

When it becomes withdrawable

Buying Stars and Telegram Premium: straight away

These orders settle immediately, so their commission is available as soon as it is credited.

Selling Stars: held for 21 days

Telegram can reclaim sold Stars for 21 days, which is the same window StarStore holds a seller's own payout for. Commission on a sell order waits out that window. If the order reverses in the meantime the commission is cancelled, because it was money StarStore never received.

Getting paid

On the 1st of each month

A payout run settles the month that has just ended. You do not request it and there is nothing to claim.

To your TON wallet, in USDT

Set the address on your ambassador account in the app. We send a reminder on the last day of the month if you have a balance and no wallet saved, and again on the 1st, because we cannot pay you without one.

Minimum 0.50 USDT

A balance below that carries over to the next run rather than being lost.

USDT on TON is free

No fee and no deduction. Other payout methods may be arranged, but their processing fees come out of your earnings, so we recommend USDT on TON.

Levels do not change this

All five levels earn the same 30%. What a level unlocks is recognition and non-cash benefits, free Stars among them, awarded by our team.
