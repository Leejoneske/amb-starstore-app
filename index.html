How It Works

The StarStore Ambassador Program works in simple steps: Share your referral link, users complete transactions, you earn commissions. Our three-tier system (Explorer, Connector, Pioneer, and Elite) offers increasing commission rates based on your performance.

Commission Rates

Explorer: 10% commission on qualifying transactions. Connector: 15% commission. Pioneer: 18% commission. Elite: 20% commission plus revenue sharing opportunities.

Payout Information

Payouts are issued monthly on the 1st of each month in USDT on the TON network. USDT transfers are free. Alternative payout methods are available but may incur processing fees. There is no minimum payout amount and no earning cap.

Eligibility Requirements

You must be 18+ years old. Have an active Telegram account. Submit a valid application through our application portal. Meet content quality standards. No large following required - we value quality engagement over follower count.

Commission Calculation

Commissions are based on completed and non-refunded transactions. Only qualifying purchases count toward your commission. Commission rates are applied automatically to all referral transactions.

Resources Available

Marketing materials, templates, swipe copy, promotional graphics. Live support through Telegram. Dedicated ambassador channel for networking. Monthly performance reports and insights. API access for integration (Elite tier). Training webinars and best practices guides.

Program Benefits

Unlimited earning potential. No startup costs or fees. Flexible work schedule. Passive income from referrals. Access to exclusive ambassador community. Priority support. Monthly payouts with no minimums. Elite-level revenue sharing opportunities. Regular performance bonuses.

FAQ

How do I join? Apply through our Telegram Mini App at https://t.me/TgStarStore_bot?startapp=ambassador or on the web at https://starstore.app/ambassador. Applications are reviewed within 1-3 business days.

How long does approval take? Most applications are reviewed within 1-3 business days. During high-volume periods, it may take up to 5 business days.

Can I be an ambassador without a large following? Yes. We value quality over quantity. Many top performers started with fewer than 500 followers.

How are commissions calculated? Commissions range from approximately 10% to 20% of qualifying transactions, based on your ambassador level. Only completed, non-refunded transactions count.

What payout methods are available? USDT on the TON network is recommended and free. Alternative methods may incur processing fees deducted from earnings. Payouts are issued on the 1st of each month with no minimum.

Is there a limit to how much I can earn? No. There is no earning cap. Elite-level ambassadors also participate in revenue sharing.

Getting Started

1. Apply through Telegram Mini App or web portal. 2. Wait for approval (1-3 business days). 3. Receive your ambassador dashboard and referral links. 4. Start sharing and earn commissions. 5. Receive monthly payouts in USDT.
