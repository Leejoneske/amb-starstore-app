How it works

Five steps, and only the third one is work.

01

Apply

Give us an email and a link to somewhere you post. We read every application and reply within 1 to 3 business days.

02

Get your link

Approval brings a referral link of your own. Anyone who joins StarStore through it is attributed to you permanently.

03

Share it

Post it wherever your audience already is. A channel, a video description, a group, a thread.

04

They trade

Your commission starts once a referred person has traded 200 Stars or bought Premium.

05

You earn

Every order they place after that adds to your balance, up to $50 from any one person.

06

You get paid

On the 1st, in USDT on TON, to the wallet on your ambassador account. No request needed.
